About the app

بنك الأسئلة الاكاديمي (Academic Question Bank) is an educational app that helps students review and prepare for exams. Its published app website describes books, references, summaries, exam questions, and interactive tests, with content for ninth-grade and baccalaureate students as well as university and major information.

Visit the app website and download information.

Google sign-in and data

The app uses Google Sign-In. Before continuing, Google's consent screen shows the requested data and permissions. Data you authorize is used for sign-in and the app features you choose, as explained in our Privacy Policy. The service does not use Google APIs to create non-consensual intimate imagery.
